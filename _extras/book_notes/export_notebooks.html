Export all Kindle highlights

Use your Amazon login. No subscription or API key required.

  1. Drag this button to your browser’s bookmarks bar:

    Export Kindle highlights

  2. Open Kindle Notebook and sign in.
  3. Click the bookmark. Keep that tab open while it visits your books and downloads one JSON file. A progress box lets you stop and save a partial export.

The script reads your highlights on Amazon and downloads them locally. It does not send them to any other service. It handles pagination and reports failed books or content Amazon cannot display. It only includes books available in the online notebook.

Move the downloaded JSON file into data/kindle/ in this repository, then run:

python3 _extras/book_notes/sync_book_notes.py

This is an on-demand export. It does not run on a schedule or bypass Amazon’s export limits.
