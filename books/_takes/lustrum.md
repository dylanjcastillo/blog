*Reading in progress.*

So far, I like this more than *Imperium*. I already knew the main characters, and it moves faster. It focuses mostly on Cicero’s year as consul and his constant fight against Caesar’s plans.

If you thought modern politics used dirty tactics, you haven’t read enough about Rome. Catilina repeatedly tried to openly kill Cicero. After failing, he even went to the Senate as if nothing had happened.

Reading this and *Imperium* taught me a lot about Cicero’s character. He came across as someone who truly cared about Rome’s wellbeing, but was also willing to compromise his principles for power.
