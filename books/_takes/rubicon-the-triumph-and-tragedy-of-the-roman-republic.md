Fun and insightful, although it can get a bit dense if you’re not familiar enough with the period and the characters. It gives you a good sense of the years leading up to the fall of the Republic.

You might blame Caesar’s ambition for the fall, but Crassus and Pompey seemed to be pursuing similar goals. It left me feeling that the Republic’s fall was almost inevitable.

I also see parallels with the US today, with people in power continually pushing to shape conditions in their own favor. Trump seemingly pushing to remove reelection limits, centralize power, and make greater use of the military came to mind while reading.
